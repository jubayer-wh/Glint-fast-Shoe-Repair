About West Irving Shoe Repair

A tradition of excellence since 1985

For over 35 years, West Irving Shoe Repair has been Chicago's trusted destination for quality shoe and leather repair. Our master craftsmen combine traditional techniques with modern technology to deliver exceptional results.

We believe that quality footwear deserves quality care. Our commitment to craftsmanship and customer satisfaction has made us the preferred choice for shoe repair in Chicago's Irving Park neighborhood and beyond.
